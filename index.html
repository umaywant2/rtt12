RTT/12 Engine

Unified Topology Synthesis

RTT/12 is the final synthesis engine in the TriadicFrameworks RTT series. It consumes the four RTT/3 operator outputs—structural topology, intersections, coherence surfaces, and GPU topology—and produces a unified RTT/12 topology.

RTT/12 is designed for regime-aware analysis, coherence–drift overlays, GPU-aligned refinement, and downstream visualization.
